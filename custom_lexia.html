COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on this 15th day of October, 2023, by and between:

Landlord: Horizon Property Management LLC, a corporation organized under the laws of Delaware, with its principal place of business at 100 Financial District Blvd, New York, NY ("Landlord").
Tenant: Nexus Technology Group Inc., a corporation organized under the laws of California, with its principal place of business at 450 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA ("Tenant").

1. Grant of Lease

Landlord, in consideration of the rent to be paid and the covenants and agreements to be performed and observed by the Tenant, does hereby lease to the Tenant and the Tenant does hereby lease and take from the Landlord the property described in Exhibit A attached hereto (the "Premises").

2. Permitted Use

The Tenant shall use and occupy the Premises solely for the purpose of operating a technology development office, general administrative functions, and related business activities. The Tenant shall not use or permit the Premises to be used for any illegal purpose or in any manner that would constitute a nuisance or disturb the quiet enjoyment of other tenants in the building. The Tenant is strictly prohibited from storing hazardous materials on the Premises.

3. Term

The term of this Lease shall be for a period of sixty (60) months, commencing on November 1, 2023 (the "Commencement Date"), and terminating on October 31, 2028 (the "Termination Date"), unless sooner terminated pursuant to any provision hereof.
